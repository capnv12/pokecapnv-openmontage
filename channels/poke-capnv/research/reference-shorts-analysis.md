Method: yt-dlp search (plus YouTube search with Shorts/short-duration filter and hashtag pages), ranked by view_count, <=180s. 12 downloaded to reference-shorts/<id>/. Frames at 0/0.5/1/2/3s and end-6/-4/-2/-0.5s (keys.jpg per folder), mid contact sheet (mid.jpg), whisper base transcript (transcript.txt). Script: extract_refs.py.

Frames were viewed at 360px width, 9 frames per video; the mid contact sheets were NOT individually inspected (middle notes come from transcripts + key frames).
