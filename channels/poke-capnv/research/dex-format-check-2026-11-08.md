# Dex format originality check (2026-11-08)

Format checked: a virtual "Dex" filled from pack openings. Each flipped card gets a verdict (Caught / Already caught / Upgraded to a higher rarity). An on-screen panel with a running counter. The run ends when the set's Dex is complete. 2-3 packs per episode, no prices, no binder.

Method: 20 YouTube searches (40 results each, about 570 unique videos), listings of the latest 60 videos and 60 Shorts for the 16 requested channels plus 23 channels that turned up in the searches, 6 web searches for Reddit and TikTok. No video was downloaded. Titles, view counts, dates and subscriber counts come from YouTube metadata; I did not watch any video.

## (a) Verdict

**YouTube.** Nobody I found runs the exact format. No channel uses the three-way verdict (Caught / Already caught / Upgraded), no channel shows an on-screen Dex panel that fills in, and none ends a run when a single set's Dex is complete. The idea of "fill a Pokédex with one card per Pokémon by opening packs" does exist, though, and nine small channels have made 3 or more videos of it. All are tiny (the biggest has 372 subscribers and 505 views on its best video), and none looks like a Shorts-style produced format. The closest:

1. **pokedex tony** (5 subscribers, physical cards): "Filling Out The Pokedex Ep. N", about 100 episodes, seen dated Mar to Jun 2025 (earlier start unchecked), mostly 2-3 minute pack openings plus 29 Shorts, with a 59-minute finale opening 50 packs. This is the same idea (species dex from packs, episode counter in the title). Each video gets 0 to 77 views. As far as the titles show there is no verdict system or panel. The finale means the series is finished.
2. **Pokédex Collector** (372 subscribers, physical): "Pokédex Challenge - how many packs to catch'em all?!", 4 long videos and about 25 Shorts from August 2026. Best video 505 views. The nearest in spirit and the newest, so it is the one to watch. It uses "catch them all" wording.
3. **Tom Ross** (1,640 subscribers, physical): "Can I Complete the Entire Pokedex From Packs Only? Pokedex Challenge Ep. 1-3", July to September 2026. 24, 12 and 29 views. Mostly a Magic and Pokémon pack channel.
4. **NerdQuest.Adventures** (5,300 subscribers, physical): "Packs to Dex: Filling the National Pokédex One Pack at a Time" and Episode 2, May 2025, 58 and 27 views. Only 2 found, so it is below your 3-video bar and may have stopped.
5. **MasterDex** (11 subscribers, physical): "Master Dex #001-#010 | Journey to 1025", June 2026, at most 371 views per video, about 20 views for #001. Species dex of all 1,025 Pokémon, from packs. Numbered episodes.
6. **KBTCGOPENS** (20 subscribers, physical): "Completing the Master Dex. Part 1 to Part 48", 68 videos and Shorts, best 1,700 views. Part 26 opens boosters and tins. "Master Dex" here most likely means a set master set, not a species dex (I could not confirm).
7. **WWEdeadman** (2,670 subscribers, physical): "Pokemon TCG Living Dex series", 6 or more videos in 2021-2023, 12 to 173 views. It is mainly a general gaming channel and the series seems abandoned. Sealed products, not a counter system.
8. **kaieqt** (about 10 subscribers): "Packdex! complete pokedex by opening packs #1, #2" (2 videos, 7 and 3 views). Below 3 videos.
9. **SingleHitGuy** (111 subscribers): "Pack to Pokedex" (2 found, 2023, about 20 to 30 views). Below 3 videos.
10. Also: **Dad & Daughter's Pokédex** (EP1-EP13, but the name is the channel brand, not a dex counter), **Frame151** (Shorts "completing the Pokedex" from packs, up to 3,000 views, only 1 long video), **JordanJapan** (2016: "The Quest to Complete the Pokedex Episode 1", old, a one-off), **PokedEX Complete** and **Arcey TCG** (1 video each).

**Big channels do the nearby ideas, but with binders and buying, not a verdict system:**
- **Deep Pocket Monster** (2.43 million subscribers) has the biggest nearby format. "Collect ALL 1,000+ Pokémon in ONE Binder" runs about 9 episodes by generation (March 2024 onward, 1.1 to 6.7 million views each) and a 4.6-hour finale for all 1,025. That is a one-card-per-species physical dex, but built from buying and trading, with a binder and prices, not pack-by-pack verdicts. His "Complete Set in N minutes or Lose It All" series is a set-completion (binder) challenge with 25+ videos, up to 25 million views.
- **Danny Phantump**: Shorts "Day N of Collecting EVERY Pokemon Card Master Set" (Day 246 to Day 274 seen, 3,200 to 18,000 views each). A daily-numbered counter, but a binder-style master set, mostly bought singles and packs.
- **Joel's Gaming Adventures**: "Pokedex Binder" upgrades, 10+ videos, up to 7,000 views. A species dex that gets upgraded to better cards, bought at shops. Closest to your "Upgraded" idea, but binder and purchase based.
- **PokeRev**: "Not Stopping Until I Pull Every Brilliant Stars / Charizard / Pikachu / Gengar Pokemon Card" (6+ videos, 0.4 to 4 million views). Pull-everything-of-one-kind challenges, no dex panel.
- **Leonhart** (2.04 million subscribers): "I Opened 151 Packs to Catch All 151 Pokemon... Or I Lose It All" (31 July 2026, 272,000 views). Closest single video to the concept (151 packs, catch all 151), but one video, not a series.
- **UnlistedLeaf, RealBreakingNate, pokecate**: "complete the set / master set / binder" videos, binder based. Others on your list (ShortPocketMonster, alexketchem, PokeDaxi, SimplyUnlucky, iPokeTCG, PokeNibbles, Purplecliffe, Ludwig) had nothing of the kind in their latest 60 videos. PokeTubers returned no videos at that handle. Ludwig has no TCG tab: his only match is the one-off "I opened every Pokémon pack ever made".

**Digital (TCG Pocket):** only tiny channels show up: Arcey TCG (1 subscriber; "Deluxe Pack EX Dex Grind - Day 1", October 2025, 7 views; a Shorts "Day 0 of my 7-day Deluxe EX Dex Challenge"), and one 4-minute "Finally completed all the Pokemon pocket card dex" video (87 views). Pocket has its own built-in dex, so that is expected. No recurring series of 3+.

**TikTok.** I could only check this through web search, which does not index TikTok well. I found nothing, which is not proof of nothing. Portal TCG and PokeRev have TikTok accounts but nothing dex-related turned up.

**Reddit and forums.** No Reddit thread for a pack-only dex series. Related: a Bulbagarden forum thread on building a Living Dex out of cards (one person, 605 Pokémon, 73 percent) and a blog post on a 1,025-card Pokédex binder with "pack pulls only" rules. These are personal projects, not video series.

**How our rules differ from every one of them.** (1) A spoken or on-screen verdict on every single card. Nobody has one. (2) Three outcomes, including "Upgraded" when a rarer version replaces the Dex entry; Joel's binder has upgrades but not as a verdict. (3) A panel that fills in, with a counter such as "61 of 94 Pokémon and trainers caught". The others count episodes ("Ep. 47") or days ("Day 272"), not progress towards a goal. (4) The run ends when one set's whole Dex is done; the others aim at all 1,025 Pokémon or at the whole TCG. (5) Packs only, with no prices and no binder; the big channels buy singles and show binders.

**Risk.** The words "Pokédex challenge", "catch them all" and "from packs only" are in use by small channels, and Pokédex Collector (August 2026) and Tom Ross (July 2026) started within the last 4 months. So the headline concept is not unique, but the mechanic (verdict plus panel plus set-by-set completion) is. No one with an audience (over 10,000 subscribers) runs a pack-opening species dex as a series.

## (c) What I could not verify

- **TikTok.** No direct search is possible with my tools; web search returned nothing relevant. A TikTok in-app search for #pokedexchallenge, #livingdex, #packdex is still needed (by a person).
- **What is inside each video.** I read titles, counts and dates only. A small channel could show a verdict or panel inside a video that the title does not mention. In particular, Pokédex Collector, Tom Ross and MasterDex should be watched once to see whether they use a counter or per-card verdicts.
- **Whether KBTCGOPENS "Master Dex" is a species dex or a set master set.** Unclear from the titles.
- **Subscriber counts** were not pulled for every channel (marked n/a), and I did not check each video's date.
- **Shorts durations** are not provided by the listing. Shorts view counts are rounded by YouTube.
- **Reddit.** Search returned no direct Reddit thread, and Reddit itself is not indexed well by the search tool, so an absence there is weak evidence. Private Instagram, Facebook and Lemon8 were not checked.
- **Titles that do not mention "dex" or "complete"** would have been missed in the search results. The 20 queries had 40 results each, so a niche channel with unusual wording could be absent.
- **PokeTubers** returned no videos at the handle I tried (@PokeTubers); the handle may be different.
