# Dex session: filming checklist

Same camera, framing, light and background as every Draw. Cards shown in hand to camera, as always. No drum, no slate, no extra holds.
Packs are opened in the order they will be published. One video = the packs of that video, in order.

## Every pack
1. Pack comes up into frame from below the bottom edge, face to camera, one smooth lift. Stop centred and hold steady 1.5 s before tearing. Never back first, never flipped. (The Dex "scans" the pack on the stop; this frame is also its cut-out in the pack line-up.)
2. Tear.
3. Every card shown one at a time, steady, straight and centred, fingers clear of the art, for at least 0.8 s before the next card. That frame is the card's landing frame and its Pokédex tile.
4. Holo or better, and any Poké Ball / Master Ball pattern card: the same, 1.5 s instead of 0.8 s.

## End of session
- Drop the file in `projects/inbox/` and tell me the session number and how many packs per video. Not the results.

## Do not
- Reorder packs after filming, or open one and film it later.
- Hurry a common: an "Already caught" needs its 0.8 s as much as a hit does.
- Put a binder, price list or phone in frame.
